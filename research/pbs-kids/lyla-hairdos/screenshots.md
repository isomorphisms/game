## Actual gameplay frames found in a parent post

A third-party parent post contains tablet photos of the running game: https://www.lemon8-app.com/@ajacymone/7466927434613490218?region=us

The visible frames show:

- a large character/hair canvas with a comb and spray bottle available as tools;
- numbered steps presented directly in the visual UI;
- a later step showing a parted/styled hairstyle with small braids;
- a close-up interaction on coiled hair strands with a large numbered step indicator;
- a pause state with Lyla/Stu still visually present.

These frames support a step-controller interpretation: the game keeps one durable hair model while swapping the tool/input logic appropriate to the current numbered step.
