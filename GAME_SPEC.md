# Dress the Unicorn game spec

This file records only rules already stated by the game designer. Open questions stay open rather than being turned into mechanics.

## Settled rules

- The game is called **Dress the Unicorn**.
- The player is a hairstylist.
- Customers include zebras, horses, and two unicorns.
- Animals wait in a waiting room; the player chooses which animal comes into the salon.
- Only one animal can be in the salon at a time.
- Choices are made by tapping, not dragging.
- The player can choose hairstyles, makeup, and dress-up items.
- Wings and horns, including rainbow horns, are for unicorns only.
- Zebras can dress up as unicorns.
- Horses are regular dress-up customers.
- Unicorns can get messed up while doing something relaxing, and then the player has to redo them.
- Zebras just get to dress up.
- At the end, the player can take photos of the animals they styled.
- There is a drink and snack shop.

### Drinks

Lemonade, pink lemonade, milk, milkshake, strawberry milkshake, coconut water, pineapple juice, banana smoothie, blueberry smoothie, strawberry smoothie, and pineapple smoothie.

### Snacks

Croissants, buns, tomatoes, cookies, cucumbers, ground cherries, carrots, mango, chips, chickpeas, soup, guacamole and chips, gummy eyeballs, gummies, candy, lollipops, Gushers, and Jolly Ranchers.

## Unanswered questions

- Which hairstyles and makeup choices should be available first?
- What relaxing thing are the unicorns doing when they get messed up?
- What exactly can a zebra use to dress up as a unicorn, given that wings and horns are stated to be for unicorns only?
- How does the drink and snack shop work beyond the stated menu?

## Smallest first playable

Use the simplest settled customer path: choose a zebra from the waiting room, put that one animal in the salon, and let the player tap a dress-up choice to change the zebra. End with the stated photo step. Do not add dragging, unicorn relaxation, horses, the shop, or resolve the zebra/unicorn-item ambiguity in this first version; those can follow without changing the basic tap-to-dress salon path.
