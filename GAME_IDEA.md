# Cheetah

## The game idea

You play as a cheetah who is running through a level. You collect all the food and avoid the bombs. If you collect all the food, you earn one star. The game has three levels to win.

## Already decided

- The player is a cheetah.
- The cheetah runs.
- Food should be collected.
- Bombs should be avoided.
- Collecting all the food earns one star.
- There are three levels.

## Still for the game designer to decide

- Does the cheetah run automatically, or does the player control every movement?
- What kinds of food are in the game?
- What happens when the cheetah touches a bomb?
- What makes each of the three levels different?
